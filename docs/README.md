# Open Understanding documentation

> **Publication lifecycle:** public-preview advisory candidate; current changes
> require human review before acceptance.
> **Authority:** advisory; [`README.adoc`](../README.adoc) is the canonical policy
> for this edition.
> **Maintenance state:** active candidate with no assigned human maintainer.
> **Human owner:** unassigned.
> **Last human review:** not reviewed.
> **Review triggers:** a canonical policy change, a public contribution, a
> material source or capability change, or a reported semantic or navigation
> defect.

This is the documentation map for the deliberately curated public edition of
Open Understanding. The high-level statement in [`README.adoc`](../README.adoc)
is authoritative for this edition.

## Start here

- [Principles](principles/README.md) -- the ideas used to assess understanding,
  evidence, review, ownership, and value.
- [Workflow](workflow/README.md) - six phases from research to human acceptance
  and merge.
- [What AI Makes Practical](what-ai-makes-practical/README.md) -- public
  operating guidance for maintained documentation, pull-request chains, and
  disposable proof-of-concept projects.
- [Practical guides](guides/README.md) -- a small adoption model for teams.

The current public cut focuses on documentation. Demonstration projects will be
considered separately when they are ready for publication review.

## Standing

This material is advisory. It should be adapted to the consequence,
reversibility, and obligations of the work. Passing a check or following a
template cannot by itself prove understanding, fitness for purpose, ownership,
or value.
