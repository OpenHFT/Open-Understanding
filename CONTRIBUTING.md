# Contributing to Open Understanding

> **Status:** public-preview contribution policy.

Review feedback, issues, and small documentation corrections are welcome during
the public preview. Please open an issue before preparing a substantial change
so its scope, authority, and relationship to the project's intent can be agreed.

Do not submit secrets, personal data, confidential or proprietary source
material, private prompts, internal links, or generated content with unclear
licensing. Use sources that a public reviewer can inspect and identify any
licensing or attribution uncertainty.

A human contributor remains accountable for submitted material, cited sources,
third-party content, review engagement, and the stated limits of the evidence.
Disclose material AI assistance and verify the claims that survive into the
proposed change.

Keep changes small enough to review as one coherent idea. Changes to canonical
`README.adoc` wording should be separated from advisory documentation changes
and require a recorded maintainer decision.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, contributions accepted into this
repository are licensed under the [Apache License, Version 2.0](LICENSE).
